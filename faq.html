About Nuru Massage
Nuru massage is an authentic Japanese massage technique that uses a special ultra-slippery gel made from natural Nori seaweed extract. The word "Nuru" means "slippery" in Japanese. The gel is transparent, odourless, tasteless, and water-soluble — and deeply nourishing for the skin. The technique involves full body contact and is renowned for being deeply relaxing and sensual.
Yes. Our Nuru gel is made from natural Nori seaweed extract — the same edible seaweed used in Japanese cuisine. It is naturally rich in iodine, calcium, and magnesium, which hydrate and nourish the skin. It is hypoallergenic, water-soluble, and washes off easily.
